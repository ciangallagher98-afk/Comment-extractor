▶ How to capture a full comments section

Fastest: the Comment Extractor Chrome extension

Open a post on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, YouTube, Threads, Bluesky or Reddit, click the extension, and press Start collecting. It scrolls the comments for you, opens the replies, and sends everything here with exact text, likes and dates — no screenshots needed. — it takes two minutes to install.

Already have comments in a spreadsheet? Use Import CSV — any file with columns for author and comment text works.

For anything else, use screenshots:

1 · Expand everything first

Anything still collapsed is invisible to the analysis. Before capturing:

  • ·Keep clicking “View more comments” until nothing more loads.
  • ·Open “View N replies” on every thread — replies are counted separately.
  • ·Click “See more” on truncated long comments, or they’ll be cut off mid-sentence.
  • ·Make sure the post itself is visible at the top — that’s where the post text is read from.

2 · Capture the scrollable area

A normal screenshot only grabs what’s on screen, and comments usually sit in their own scrolling box. This extension captures that box in full:

Get “Full Page Scrollable Area” ↗
  • ·Point it at the comments container, not the whole window — that’s the part that scrolls independently.
  • ·One capture is usually enough. For very long threads, take several overlapping captures and upload them in order — repeats at the seams are merged automatically.
  • ·Up to 6 images per run. Images are shrunk in your browser before upload.

      Install the Chrome extension

      Collects a post and all its comments from TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, YouTube, Threads, Bluesky and Reddit, and sends them here.

      Download the extension
      1. 1 Unzip the download and move the comment-extractor-extension folder somewhere you'll keep it, like Documents. Chrome runs it from there, so don't delete it.
      2. 2 Open a new tab and go to chrome://extensions (copy it into the address bar).
      3. 3 Turn on Developer mode with the switch in the top-right corner.
      4. 4 Click Load unpacked and choose the comment-extractor-extension folder.
      5. 5 Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin Comment Extractor, then reload any social media tabs you have open.
      Updating? Download the new version, replace the files in your existing folder, then click the circular reload arrow on the Comment Extractor card in chrome://extensions.

      To use it: open a post on its own page, click the extension, and press Start collecting. The results open here automatically.
