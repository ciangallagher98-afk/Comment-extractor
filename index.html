▶ How to capture a full comments section

1 · Expand everything first

Anything still collapsed is invisible to the analysis. Before capturing:

  • ·Keep clicking “View more comments” until nothing more loads.
  • ·Open “View N replies” on every thread — replies are counted separately.
  • ·Click “See more” on truncated long comments, or they’ll be cut off mid-sentence.
  • ·Make sure the post itself is visible at the top — that’s where the post text is read from.

2 · Capture the scrollable area

A normal screenshot only grabs what’s on screen. Use a scrolling-capture extension:

Get a full-page screenshot extension ↗

GoFullPage, Awesome Screenshot and Fireshot all work — any of them captures the whole scrollable region in one image.

  • ·Very long threads: take several overlapping captures and upload them in order. Repeats at the seams are merged automatically.
  • ·Up to 8 images per run. Images are shrunk in your browser before upload.
